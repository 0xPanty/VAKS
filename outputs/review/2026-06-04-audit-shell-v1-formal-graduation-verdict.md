# Audit Shell v1 Formal Graduation Verdict

## Verdict

```text
ready_for_formal_operator_only_operation_convention
```

说人话：现在可以毕业。

毕业范围是：后台 operator-only operation convention。不是 schema，不是 truth layer，不是用户界面，不是自动真值系统。

## Why This Is Now Enough

Initial 80/60/60 pilot passed the hard gates. The high-intensity supplement closed the previous narrow gaps:

- Full 282-row triage passed.
- Blind no-`first_action` triage passed.
- Deferred promotion trigger matrix passed.
- Exact selector-grade evidence-anchor click-back passed for eligible evidence packets.
- Weak legacy evidence packets were detected and quarantined instead of being overclaimed.
- Non-evidence landing negative controls passed.
- Source-family adversarial matrix passed.
- Direct-full-audit burden proxy passed.

## Found Issue

67 legacy alpha evidence packets have anchors that are too weak or not usable as selector-grade evidence landing. They are not blockers for the operator convention because the rule is to quarantine them, not trust them.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

- `deferred_review_unless_promoted` requires promotion receipt before citation, action-basis, rule/convention basis, explicit challenge, conflict handling, or weak-anchor answer impact.
- Receipt not generated means no action-basis.
- Anchor-strength strip is front-face summary only.
- Only selector-grade jump-back can claim evidence landing.
- Source, claim, context, diagnostic, history, binary, transcript, dashboard, test, lint, count, lineage, and feedback are not evidence support by themselves.
- Recurrence / grouping remains default-off.

## Remaining Non-Blockers

Future UI polish, human reviewer ergonomics, or recurrence/grouping experiments are separate work and do not block this formal operator-only convention.
