Landed capabilities

  • Receipts and hash boundariesRetrieval and operation outputs now carry inspectable receipts and source/hash boundaries.
  • Validator failure matrixValidators are paired with negative cases so green checks cannot hide known boundary failures.
  • Read-only query and retrieval pilotsQuery and retrieval pilots remain inspect-only and cannot create truth, evidence, or action authority.
  • Formal-vault preflight dry runsFormal-vault work is represented as dry-run packets and gates, not as actual vault access.
  • Operator status and workflow surfacesOperators can inspect status, next steps, approval requirements, and stop conditions.
  • Follow-up-agent blind-run regressionIndependent agents are tested against prompts to catch authority confusion before continuation.
  • Rollup and decision brief surfacesCompleted sandbox slices are summarized into readable rollups and decision briefs.

Positive improvements

  • Retrieval no longer counts as supportFinding a source is separated from proving that it supports a point.
  • Sandbox pass no longer counts as mainlineProduct-grade sandbox artifacts remain outside main architecture until separately approved.
  • Validator pass no longer counts as approvalAutomated validation is evidence for review, not reviewer or product acceptance.
  • Agent decision no longer counts as action basisAgent judgments are constrained to sandbox observations unless a separate authority exists.
  • Operator can see pass, verification, and revision statusThe rollup exposes Product PASS counts, verification coverage, and revision history.
  • Boundary crossing requires explicit separate approvalFormal vault, promotion, merge, commit, push, sync, and action-basis use remain blocked.
