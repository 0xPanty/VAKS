## Workbench Lanes

### Capture Now

- Primary action: drop source, note, transcript, or repo into quarantine
- Boundary: raw/candidate only; not reviewed knowledge

### Triage Status

- Primary action: see blocked, stale, dirty, and missing-anchor states
- Boundary: status display only; does not approve

### Review Radar

- Primary action: route candidates with gaps into review pressure
- Boundary: review_due is pressure, not truth mutation

### Receipt Center

- Primary action: inspect hash, source, operation, and product acceptance receipts
- Boundary: hash receipt is process integrity, not evidence support

### Action Boundary

- Primary action: stop before action until action readiness and sufficiency pass
- Boundary: this workbench is not action basis

### Next Safe Step

- Primary action: show the one bounded next move
- Boundary: next step is guidance only; no commit, no push, no sync
