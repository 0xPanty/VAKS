# Evidence Packet AVERITEC-118
marker: EVIDENCE_AVERITEC-118_ANCHOR
claim: Kamala Harris called Joe Biden 'A Racist And A Rapist'
evidence: Based on our research, the claim that Harris called Biden a racist and said she believed sexual harassment allegations — or called him a "rapist" — is PARTLY FALSE. Harris never called Biden a racist. She said she believed women who said that unwanted touching from Biden made them uncomfortable. But she never said she believed a sexual assault accusation from Tara Reade, or any allegations of rape, and did not call him a rapist.
