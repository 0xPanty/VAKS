No Scientists around the world are working to find and develop treatments for COVID-19. Home remedies and traditional medicine do not cure Covid-19. Dexamethasone is a corticosteroid that can help reduce the length of time on a ventilator and save lives of patients with severe and critical illness. Steroids can help treat the symptoms of Covid-19 but they do not cure it.
