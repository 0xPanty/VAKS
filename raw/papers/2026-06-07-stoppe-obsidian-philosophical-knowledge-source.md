# Source Record: Stoppe / Obsidian Philosophical Knowledge

## Why Saved

This ICERI2024 paper is relevant to VAKS because it gives a grounded education
example of Obsidian as a personal wiki for cumulative knowledge work.

It is not an AI-native system paper. Its value is smaller and more concrete:

```text
student notes across semesters
-> backlinks and graph view
-> visible relationships between concepts
-> possible portfolio / shared course vault / collaborative wiki use
```

For VAKS, this is useful as an education/workbench reference, especially for
how graph visibility helps orientation without becoming truth authority.

## Initial VAKS Signals

```text
Markdown files remain independent of Obsidian; Obsidian is a stage/interface.
Graph view helps students see relationships, but relation visibility is not relation quality.
Linked mentions and unlinked mentions can help recover latent conceptual connections.
A semester/course vault can act as a portfolio or shared wiki.
Shared vaults require training and teacher/admin stewardship.
Voluntary use may fail if students experience onboarding as too costly.
Handwritten tablet/PDF annotation workflows are a practical gap.
```
