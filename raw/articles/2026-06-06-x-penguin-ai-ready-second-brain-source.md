## What The Source Says

The article argues that a second brain should not only store notes. It should
store enough context for AI to recognize the user's patterns:

```text
ideas
observations
decisions
mistakes
projects
prompts
workflows
rules
taste
examples
repeated patterns
```

Its proposed note format asks:

```text
What is this?
Why does it matter?
Where can I use it?
What does it connect to?
What question does it create?
```

## Source-Useful Details

Reusable details worth routing as candidate comprehension:

- AI output improves when it can see user context, taste, constraints, history,
  and repeated patterns.
- Notes should contain why they matter, not only what happened.
- Mistakes, decisions, prompts, workflows, and personal rules are useful context
  categories.
- Weekly review can ask AI to find repeated patterns and turn useful patterns
  into projects, decisions, or prompts.
- Noise should be archived or deleted for activation hygiene.
