## What The Source Says

The article argues that a bloated vault can degrade AI-assisted synthesis
because low-value captures dilute the context an agent reasons across.

Its main distinction is:

```text
captured information is not knowledge;
thinking is what the user did with information;
thinking compounds;
unprocessed clippings compound into noise.
```

The author recommends removing:

```text
saved articles never reprocessed
highlights with no reaction attached
quick captures never developed
duplicate versions of the same thinking
```

The author also recommends a reversible first pass:

```text
use Claude to classify KEEP / DELETE / REVIEW;
do not let Claude delete;
move deletion candidates to an archive first;
wait before permanent deletion.
```

## Source-Useful Details

Reusable details worth routing as candidate comprehension:

- Signal density affects the usefulness of AI retrieval and synthesis.
- A vault is an attention surface, not just a storage surface.
- Notes containing the user's reaction, thesis, analysis, or active question are
  more valuable than unprocessed collected information.
- Duplicate thinking can be consolidated into the sharpest version.
- Rare hard-to-refind data points may be retained in minimal form.
- Cleanup prompts should suggest candidates, not perform destructive actions.
- Archive / quarantine before deletion is safer than direct deletion.
