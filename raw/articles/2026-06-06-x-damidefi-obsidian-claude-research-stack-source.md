# X Source: Dami-Defi Obsidian + Claude Research Stack Thread

## Boundary

This source card preserves a user-provided X article / thread about replacing a
multi-tool research stack with Obsidian and Claude.

It is not reviewed knowledge. It is not a tool adoption decision. It does not
prove that Readwise, Telegram bots, Zapier, Make, n8n, Claude Projects, or the
listed folder structure should be adopted by VAKS.

## What The Source Says

The article argues that the user's previous stack had five isolated tools:

```text
Notion
Readwise
X bookmarks
browser bookmarks
ChatGPT
```

The claimed problem was not tool quality in isolation. It was connection cost:
highlights, links, projects, AI chats, and outputs did not meet in one
environment.

The proposed replacement is:

```text
one Obsidian vault
one fast Inbox
processed Sources
atomic Ideas
active Projects
finished Outputs
Claude context / prompts / logs
weekly synthesis across recent notes
source templates with connection and open-question fields
```

## Source-Useful Details

Reusable details worth routing as candidate comprehension:

- A fragmented research stack creates hidden connection cost.
- Fast capture matters; the source gives a practitioner threshold of under ten
  seconds.
- Everything lands in Inbox first and gets processed later.
- Source notes use consistent fields so Claude can reason across sources.
- The weekly prompt asks for connections, patterns, contradictions, and the
  highest-value source, not a summary.
- Contradictions are listed without automatic resolution.
- Capture adapters such as Readwise plugin or Telegram bot are just ingestion
  routes into the vault.
- Claude context is useful when it sees active project notes and source notes,
  but that does not make its outputs reviewed.
