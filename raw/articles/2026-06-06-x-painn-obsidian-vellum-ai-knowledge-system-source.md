## What The Source Says

The article proposes a four-tool stack:

```text
Obsidian:
  local Markdown vault

Vellum:
  intelligence layer that reads, connects, and synthesizes notes

Readwise:
  highlight aggregation and Obsidian sync

N8N:
  automation layer for background capture and workflows
```

Its vault structure includes:

```text
00-INBOX
01-CAPTURES/articles
01-CAPTURES/ideas
01-CAPTURES/patterns
01-CAPTURES/questions
01-CAPTURES/numbers
02-CONNECTIONS
03-PROJECTS
04-VELLUM/VELLUM.md
04-VELLUM/workflows
```

The source emphasizes organizing captures by type rather than topic so that
cross-domain connections can surface.

## Source-Useful Details

Reusable details worth routing as candidate comprehension:

- Role-separated stack: vault, intelligence layer, capture sources, automation.
- Capture automation reduces decision friction.
- Type-based capture folders may help cross-domain connection discovery.
- `VELLUM.md` is a read-first steering file for identity, project context, and
  desired behavior.
- Workflow files act as visible operating procedures.
- Daily brief, weekly connections, and deep research outputs are synthesized
  candidate outputs.
- Connection types include same principle, contradiction/tension, multi-note
  pattern, and accidental answer.
- Closing the loop records whether beliefs, patterns, or connections later
  played out.
