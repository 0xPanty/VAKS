## What The Source Says

The article argues that Obsidian setups fail when they start from imagined
organization rather than actual behavior. Its recommended order is:

```text
capture first
minimum structure second
plugins only after real problems
review loop to resurface what matters
```

The proposed vault starts small:

```text
00 - Inbox
01 - Notes
02 - Projects
03 - Resources
```

It also proposes daily notes, a weekly review, retrieval-friendly note names,
and a small plugin set.

## Source-Useful Details

Reusable details worth routing as candidate comprehension:

- Friction at capture time prevents use.
- An Inbox removes the decision of where a thought belongs.
- Folder structure should be minimal until a real limitation appears.
- Daily notes can be a working surface rather than a diary.
- Weekly review prevents captured notes from becoming slow forgetting.
- Random note review can reactivate older material.
- Note names should optimize retrieval rather than filing aesthetics.
- Links are useful connection bets but not truth.
